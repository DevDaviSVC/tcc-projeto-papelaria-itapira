Born from a Doodle

What started as a shared passion for colorful notebooks and smooth-writing pens in a tiny garage studio is now Papelaria Criativa. We believe that everyone is an artist at heart, and our mission is to provide the spark that ignites your creativity.

Every item in our collection is handpicked or designed with a simple goal: to make the act of creating as joyful as the result itself. From neon markers to eco-friendly planners, we bring the fun back to functionality.
